How to Play Scoundrel

Overview

Scoundrel is a solo dungeon-crawl card game. Manage your health, weapons, and potions to survive against monsters and clear the dungeon!

Card Types

  • Monsters (♣/♠): Values 2-10, J(11), Q(12), K(13), A(14). They deal damage equal to their value.
  • Weapons (♦ 2-10): Reduce damage from monsters. Discard old weapon when equipping new one.
  • Potions (♥ 2-10): Heal by their value, capped at 20. Only one potion per room.

Gameplay

  1. Each turn, draw cards until 4 are visible (the Room).
  2. Choose to Avoid (put all 4 cards at bottom of deck) or Face the room.
  3. When facing, resolve 3 cards in any order. The 4th card carries to the next room.
  4. You cannot avoid twice in a row.

Combat Rules

  • Bare-handed: Take full monster value as damage.
  • With weapon: Damage = monster value - weapon value (min 0).
  • Weapon rule: A weapon can only fight monsters with value ≤ its last defeated monster.
  • Example: Weapon defeats a 7, then can only fight monsters ≤ 7.

Winning & Losing

  • Win: Clear all cards from the deck. Score = remaining health.
  • Lose: Health drops to 0. Score = -(sum of remaining monster values).

Tips

  • Plan your card order carefully - the 4th card carries over!
  • Sometimes avoiding is better than taking damage.
  • Keep track of which monsters your weapon can still fight.
